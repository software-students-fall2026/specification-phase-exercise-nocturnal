## Review of the Current Application

# Strength

1. The Slide Machine does well at pulling out the key information from lecturer's speech, discarding stutters and rambling.
2. The generated texts are better organized than lecturer's own notes (seed material).
3. The generated slides are well formatted, wihtout overcrowding and is visually pleasing for viewers.

# Weakness

4. The photos generated by the lecturer are not always accurate and may not reflect the intended meaning.
5. Some Exit Quiz questions only feature answer options that were never mentioned by the lecterur. This can confuse viewers, as they are being tested on information that wasn't taught.
6. Some Exit Quiz questions are drawm from a term the lecturer mentioned in passing. The question may not reflect the core concept the lecterur wants the viewer to take away from the lecture.
7. The slide generator sometimes splits a single idea across two slides, breaking up material that was intended to belong together.

# Gap

8. There is no way for the lecturer to hold the current slide in place while still speaking about it.
9. There is no live way for a viewer to follow a lecture on their own device as the slides are generated.
10. Lecturers are unable to edit the slides by voice after the lecture ends.
