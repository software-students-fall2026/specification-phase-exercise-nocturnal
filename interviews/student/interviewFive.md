Christina Lin conducted a closed interview with Bennie – a philosophy and environmental science major. She split the interview into three phases: before using “The Slide Machine”, observing first-time application use, and initial reactions after. 

Bennie was provided limited information during the initial interview stage. Christina asked about his goals, needs and desires as a student. Bennie described wanting a time effective solution for lecture organization to avoid wasting time on ancillary material. He expressed frustration when lecture slides don’t contain core content. Bennie was also asked about his experience as a presenter. He voiced his struggle to find effective visualization tools for environmental systems and philosophy arguments, the struggle of curating and organizing information before presentations, difficulty creating a standardized style system for slides, etc.

Christina noted the following observations while Bennie trialed the app:
- “The Slide Machine” handled stutters well.
- Seeded images selected during live generation were consistently incorrect. 
- Slides were created during pauses (even unintentional ones). 
- Does well at pulling out important, relevant information. Specifically observed when Bennie specifically tested interleaving gibberish with actual lecture content. 
- Bennie did generate an exit ticket quiz.

In the post-use debrief, Bennite was quite impressed. He noted every piece of content generated was unique (no repeated information), slides were generated in consistent style throughout, slides were visually pleasing with little overcrowding. His only frustration with “The Slide Machine” was the image selection during live generation. The exit ticket quiz was deemed adequate. 

Goals / Needs
- Organize lecture material efficiently. 
- Avoid spending time on ancillary or irrelevant material.
- Ensure slides contain the core content of a lecture. 
- Find effective ways to visualize environmental systems. 
- Represent philosophy arguments clearly. 
- Curation and organization of information before presenting.
- Maintaining a standardized visual style throughout the presentation. 

Problems / Frustrations
- Creating and organizing presentation content manually is time-consuming. 
- Seeded images selected during the live presentation were incorrect. 
- Slides were created during pauses, including unintentional ones.
