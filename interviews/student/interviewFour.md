Jason Wang conducted an open interview with Robert – an art student. Robert was asked what he would want from a platform like “The Slide Machine”. As an art student, Robert hoped generated slides could have multiple images, flexible layouts, and relevant visuals that correctly populate with different visual treatments applied depending on the active topic of discussion. After experimenting with the product, Robert felt the current templates were too restrictive for image-heavy presentations, that correct visuals didn’t always populate, and automatically generated visuals didn’t match the current topic of discussion.  

Goals / Needs:
- Place multiple images together on a single slide.
- Compare artwork and visual references side by side. 
- Use flexible layouts suited to art and design subjects. 
- Receive automatically generated visuals that are relevant to the current topic. 
- Different “visual treatments” based on the nature of current content. 

Problems / Frustrations: 
- Current templates feel too restrictive for image-heavy presentations. 
- Automatically generated visuals do not consistently match the topic being discussed. 
- Incorrect visual selection reduces the accuracy of otherwise relevant slides.
